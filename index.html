About me

I build scrapers, indexers, and the apps that sit on top. Most of my side projects start with public data that's annoying to read — a city issue tracker, an Instagram story, an exam office website — and end as a small app that makes it readable.

I work across the stack in TypeScript, from the scraper and database to the interface, and I host what I build myself.
